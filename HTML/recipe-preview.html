Creamy Garlic Pasta

A delicious and creamy pasta dish made with fresh garlic, parmesan cheese, and a rich creamy sauce. Perfect for a quick and tasty meal.

Ingredients

  • Spaghetti 250 g
  • Garlic 4 cloves
  • Butter 2 tablespoons
  • Heavy Cream 1 cup
  • Parmesan Cheese 1/2 cup
  • Salt and Black Pepper To taste

Cooking Instructions

  1. Boil the spaghetti in salted water until it becomes tender.
  2. Melt butter in a pan and add minced garlic. Cook until fragrant.
  3. Add heavy cream and stir gently.
  4. Add parmesan cheese, salt, and black pepper.
  5. Add the cooked spaghetti and mix everything together.
  6. Serve hot and enjoy your creamy garlic pasta!
