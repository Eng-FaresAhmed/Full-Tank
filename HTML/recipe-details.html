Creamy Garlic Parmesan Pasta

★★★★★ 634 Reviews

A silky pasta with garlic and parmesan sauce. Ready in about 25 minutes.

Ingredients

Ingredient Quantity
Fettuccine pasta400 g
Garlic cloves4
Heavy cream1 cup
Parmesan cheese100 g
Butter2 tbsp
Fresh parsley2 tbsp
Salt and black pepperTo taste

Instructions

  1. Boil the pasta in salted water until al dente, then drain and keep half a cup of pasta water.
  2. Melt the butter in a pan and cook the minced garlic for one minute until fragrant.
  3. Pour in the cream and let it simmer gently for three minutes.
  4. Stir in the parmesan until the sauce is smooth and thick.
  5. Add the pasta and toss well, adding a little pasta water if the sauce is too thick.
  6. Season, sprinkle with parsley, and serve hot.
